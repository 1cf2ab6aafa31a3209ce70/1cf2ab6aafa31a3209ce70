- Content: the title name Few Tiles, chosen by the project owner on 2026-10-21; menu, play, teaching, tray and result text; 30 original boards in three chapters; and SpriteKit tiles drawn as rounded squares in the system background color with one SF Symbol per kind in a system color (Leaf green, Drop blue, Sun orange, Moon indigo, Star yellow, Heart pink). The [app icon](Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset) is the scaffold's placeholder, rendered by `scripts/app-icon.py --frames tile-match`; a title icon is a [Polishing](../../roadmap/polishing.md) item. There are no imported images, sounds, fonts or translations; shell feedback uses the shared authored tones.
- Rules: [ADR-011](../../roadmap/decisions/ADR-011-tile-match-rules.md). Select a free tile to move it into the seven-slot tray; three alike in the tray clear. A tile is free when no tile on a higher layer overlaps it. A board is cleared when the board and tray are empty; a full tray stays in play with Undo and End board. Each triple scores the empty tray slots it leaves, and only a cleared board's score counts toward the level's best.
- Creator and date: project contributors using Claude Code, 2026-10-21. This is original material authored for this repository; no upstream code, levels, artwork or names were used as inputs. Tray triple-matching over layered tiles is a common mechanic, not copied expression.
- Creation method: `intro-01` and `intro-02` are written by hand (seeds 301 and 302 only identify them); the authoring solver records a clearing sequence for each. The other 28 boards are generated by reverse construction from the seeds in the level plan: the generator thins a centered pyramid of tile positions symmetrically, draws an order in which each tile is free when its turn comes, and gives kinds along that order so that at most one to three kinds wait in the tray at once, so each board ships with a clearing sequence that never fills the tray. Each generated board also meets its plan's difficulty band: the share of 1,000 uniformly random plays that clear it, which falls from about 0.68 on `intro-03` to about 0.001 on `final-10`. `TileContentValidator generate Games/TileContent/level-plan.json <directory>` rebuilds every level and the catalog, and `test-content-cli.py` checks that the result matches the bundled files byte for byte. The random-play rate orders difficulty; it is not a measure of how hard people find a board, which needs play testing.
